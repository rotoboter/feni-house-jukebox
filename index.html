Feni House Jukebox

The song-request jukebox at Feni House & Taberna, Anjuna, Goa.

Guests choose the music: they search for a song at the jukebox by the bar, or on their phone by scanning the code on the table, and it joins the night's queue. Staff run the queue from a dashboard.

The jukebox uses the venue's own YouTube account to keep each night's requests in a YouTube playlist, which is played on the screen in the bar. It asks for access to that one account only, and only to create and manage those playlists.
