Privacy policy

Feni House Jukebox · Last updated 17 October 2026

The Feni House Jukebox is run by Feni House & Taberna in Anjuna, Goa, for its own venue. This page explains what it collects and why.

The venue's YouTube account

The jukebox asks the venue's own Google account for permission to manage its YouTube account (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). It uses that permission only to create one playlist per night, add the songs guests request, remove songs staff take out of the queue, read what is in that playlist, and look up song titles and lengths. It does not post, comment, subscribe, upload or change anything else.

The sign-in token is stored only on the venue's own computer that runs the jukebox. It is never sold or shared. The venue can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The jukebox's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Guests

  • Requests: the songs requested, and when, are kept so the queue works and the venue can see what is popular.
  • Names: a guest may add a first name or nickname to a request. It is shown on the screens in the bar and never on other guests' phones. It is optional.
  • Phones: a phone that scans the table code is given an anonymous random code in a cookie, so the jukebox can apply its fair-use limits (a few requests per phone each night). It contains no personal information and is not used for advertising or tracking.
  • The jukebox does not ask guests to sign in and does not collect phone numbers, emails or locations.

Listening history

The venue may import its own YouTube watch history (from Google Takeout) to see what the room plays. It stays on the venue's computer and is used only for the venue's own reports and song suggestions.

Where data is kept

On the computer that runs the jukebox at the venue. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with third parties except YouTube itself, as described above.
